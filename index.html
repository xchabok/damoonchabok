Damoon Chabok

I build things that sit somewhere between software, art and questions that refuse to go away.

Some projects become applications. Some become images. Some become books. Most begin as an attempt to understand a problem that continues to follow me long after the initial curiosity has disappeared.

My work explores survival, desire, failure, persistence, perception and the strange rules of a game none of us agreed to play.

This website is a collection of finished works, ongoing projects and unfinished investigations.

Selected Work

The Target
An artistic exploration of pursuit, distance and the moving nature of goals.

Septets
A completed artistic project built around narrative, imagery and conceptual experimentation.

We Must Not Be Looted
A book project examining survival, suffering, achievement and the rules imposed on existence.

Everything here is part of a larger attempt to understand what people do, why they continue, and what remains after the reasons are gone.
