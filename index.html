Damoon Chabok

Generative artist. Programmer. Writer of unfinished philosophical notes disguised as books.

Statement

I build systems that behave like thoughts and texts that behave like broken systems. What I make usually starts as a technical problem and ends as something closer to a question that refuses resolution.

Practices

— Generative visual systems
— Conceptual software
— Experimental writing
— Narrative structures built from failure, repetition, and persistence

Current Focus

A philosophical street-level book exploring survival, desire, exhaustion, and the absurd logic of continuing without agreement. Alongside that: generative visual experiments that treat code as a living instability.

Everything here is an attempt to understand why things continue even when meaning becomes unclear.
